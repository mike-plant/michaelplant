*Last updated: September 2026*

Short version: I only collect what you choose to send me, I use it to reply to you, and I don't sell it.

## What gets collected

**Forms.** When you fill out a form on this site (for example, the Lakewood neighborhood form or a market-report request), I receive what you typed: usually your name, email, and whatever details you add. The form also records which page you were on and, if you arrived from a link or a card, where you came from. That helps me understand what's actually useful.

{% if site.hubspot.portalId -%}
Form submissions are stored in HubSpot, the tool I use to keep track of conversations and follow-ups.
{%- else -%}
Form submissions reach me by email.
{%- endif %}

**Scheduling.** The contact page embeds Calendly. If you book a time, Calendly handles that information under [its own privacy policy](https://calendly.com/privacy).

**Listings search.** Listing search links open on my {{ site.brokerage.name }} agent site, which has its own privacy policy.

This site doesn't use advertising trackers and doesn't sell or rent your information to anyone.

## How it's used

To reply to you, to follow up on what you asked about, and to understand which pages are useful. If you asked about real estate, your information may be shared with {{ site.brokerage.name }} as required for me to work with you as a licensed salesperson.

Personal and faith conversations are kept out of any business or marketing system.

## Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct, or delete anything you've sent. Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and I'll take care of it.

If you get an email from me and don't want more, reply and say so.
