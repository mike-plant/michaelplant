I take on a small number of exterior projects on older Lakewood and west-side homes. Usually about one a month, so I can do each one right.

## What I take on

- Porch and deck boards, stairs, and limited framing repair
- Railings, handrails, posts, and columns
- Exterior trim, fascia, soffit, and rot repair
- Fence and gate repair, small exterior carpentry
- Exterior doors and wood storm doors
- Inspection and pre-sale punch-list items

## What I usually don't

Interiors, full remodels, roofing, and anything that needs a general contractor's crew. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you quickly and point you somewhere better if I can.

## How to start

The fastest way is a text with a few photos, your address, and roughly when you'd like it done. A budget range helps but isn't required.
