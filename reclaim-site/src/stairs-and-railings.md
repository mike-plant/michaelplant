## When steps need more than paint

- A tread flexes or dips when you step near the middle
- The bottom of the stringers sits on soil or a cracked pad and looks dark or soft
- Steps pull away from the porch or lean to one side
- The handrail moves when you lean on it, or there isn't one
- Risers are uneven, so people trip on the same step

Stairs fail from the bottom up. The part that rots first is usually the part you can't see.

## Materials

Painted wood or cellular PVC on front steps of older houses, so the profiles match. Composite treads can make sense on back stairs. Pressure-treated framing everywhere, kept off the dirt. Your written price lists each option and what it costs.
