## What it usually costs

Most porch repairs I do run **$1,800 to $4,500**. A small floor section or a single column base is at the low end. A sagging beam with two columns is at the high end. Those are planning ranges, not quotes. Your price is fixed in writing after I see it.

## Repair or replace

I'd rather save original wood than tear it out. Old-growth lumber outlasts almost anything sold today, and original porch details are hard to match. When a board or column has failed all the way through, I replace it. When it hasn't, I repair it. I'll show you which is which.

## Materials, and when I use each

- **Wood** for anything you see up close on a front porch: columns, trim, railings, tongue and groove floors. It matches the original profiles and takes paint the way the rest of the house does.
- **Cellular PVC** (Azek and similar) for parts that sit in water: column bases, the bottom rail, trim near the floor. It won't rot, and painted, it reads like wood.
- **Composite** (Trex and similar) for back porch and deck floors, where low upkeep matters more than matching 1915.

Your written price lists the material for each part and why.

Some porch work needs a city permit. I'll tell you up front and handle it.
