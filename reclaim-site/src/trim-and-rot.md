## What I repair

- **Fascia and soffit.** Rotted fascia boards, soffit panels, and the rafter tails behind them. Usually caused by gutters that overflow or pull away.
- **Trim, sills, and corners.** Window and door casings, sills, corner boards, water tables.
- **Decks.** Board replacement, deck railings and stairs, limited reframing on existing decks.

## How I decide what to do

I probe every soft spot with an awl before I price anything.

- **Consolidate and fill** when the rot is shallow and the board is otherwise sound, especially on original moulded trim that's hard to match.
- **Dutchman patch** when one section of a long board has failed. That's the repair in the Reclaim mark.
- **Replace** when rot runs through the board or into the framing.

I'll also tell you what caused it. If it's a gutter, roof, or grading problem, the repair won't last until that's fixed, and I'll say so.
