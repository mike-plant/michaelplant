The shop that became the neighborhood's living room

How one small business on the west end of Madison turned a storefront into a community anchor — and what they're planning next.

This isn't just where I come to shop. This is where I come to find out what's happening in my neighborhood.

— A Regular

When they opened the door on a cold January morning three years ago, they didn't know if anyone would come. Now, the shop on the 1200 block of Madison Avenue is standing room only on Saturday afternoons.

It started with a simple idea: bring the things the neighborhood was missing to the neighborhood that was asking for them. Local goods, local art, local knowledge.

The owners have become de facto historians of the west end, tracking who moves in, what closes, what the street needs. Their chalkboard calendar is often the most reliable event listing in the district.
