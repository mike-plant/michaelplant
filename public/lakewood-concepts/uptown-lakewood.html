About the Alliance

Building community,
one storefront at a time.

The Uptown Lakewood Business Alliance has connected the west end of Madison Avenue since 2007. We're a coalition of independent business owners working to make this stretch of street the most vibrant, welcoming corridor in Lakewood.

Connect Businesses

Networking, shared promotion, and collective advocacy for the district's independent operators.

Guide Visitors

Helping people discover the west end — where to eat, shop, explore, and spend an afternoon.

Engage Community

Events, initiatives, and partnerships that make the neighborhood feel like home.

Help New Business

Resources and support for entrepreneurs ready to open on Madison Avenue.

Business Spotlight

The Wandering
Lantern

Tucked into the heart of Uptown Lakewood, The Wandering Lantern is the kind of shop you didn't know you needed until you stepped inside. Part gift shop, part curiosity cabinet, part neighborhood gathering point — it's become one of the west end's most beloved stops.

"We wanted to create a place that felt like discovering something. Every shelf should surprise you, and every visit should feel like the first time." — Owner, The Wandering Lantern

Open since joining the district, The Wandering Lantern has become a fixture of the Uptown Lakewood experience — proof that independent retail isn't just surviving on Madison Avenue, it's thriving.

Join the Alliance

Your business belongs
on this street.

ULBA membership means visibility, community, and a seat at the table. Whether you've been here for years or just signed a lease, we'd love to have you.

Members get directory listings, event participation, social media features, and a voice in the future of the district. Annual dues are structured to be accessible for businesses of all sizes.

Start a Business Here

Ready to open
on Madison Avenue?

Lakewood is one of the most business-friendly cities in Northeast Ohio, and the west end of Madison has available storefronts, foot traffic, and a built-in community of fellow entrepreneurs ready to welcome you.

Lakewood Alive

Lakewood's community development organization offers free technical assistance, storefront improvement grants, and small business support programs for entrepreneurs launching in the city.

Learn more
City of Lakewood

The city's economic development team can walk you through permits, zoning, licensing, and available incentive programs for new businesses in the Madison Avenue corridor.

ULBA Mentorship

Connect with established west end business owners who've been in your shoes. Our members are happy to share advice on everything from lease negotiations to neighborhood marketing.
