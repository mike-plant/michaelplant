Who We Are

A real alliance for a
real neighborhood.

The Uptown Lakewood Business Alliance has been the connective tissue of the west end of Madison Avenue since 2007. We're the businesses here — independently owned, deeply local, and genuinely invested in each other's success.

We organize events that bring people to the district, shine a spotlight on our members, help newcomers navigate the city, and make sure Uptown Lakewood has a strong voice in the decisions that shape our community.

Whether you're a visitor discovering the west end for the first time, or a business owner looking to put down roots here, we're here to connect you to everything this neighborhood has to offer.

The Wandering Lantern
This Month's Feature

A shop that became the
neighborhood's living room.

Some businesses sell things. The Wandering Lantern sells the feeling that you found exactly what you didn't know you were looking for. Tucked into the west end of Madison Avenue, it has become a destination in the truest sense — a place people return to, recommend, and bring their out-of-town guests.

"This isn't just where I come to shop. This is where I come to find out what's happening in my neighborhood."

— A Regular

The Wandering Lantern is a founding member of the Uptown Lakewood Business Alliance and a cornerstone of what the west end is becoming.
